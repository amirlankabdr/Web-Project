01 / Understand the basics

Three parts worth understanding

These appear in almost every computer specification.

CPU

Processor

Carries out instructions and calculations. It helps run the operating system and your programs.

In class: compiling a program or calculating a spreadsheet.

RAM

Memory

Holds data that running programs need. It is temporary and clears when power is removed.

In class: keeping a browser, editor, and video call open together.

SSD / HDD

Storage

Keeps your files and applications. An SSD generally loads files faster than a hard drive.

In class: saving projects, lecture recordings, and software.

03 / Before you decide

Common questions

A few things to check before choosing a computer.

It depends on your courses. Documents and browsing usually do not require gaming hardware. Check the requirements of any specialist software you will use.

Many desktops allow component upgrades. Laptop options vary, and some have soldered memory. Check the exact model before assuming a part can be replaced.

Consider a monitor, keyboard, mouse, headphones, and any software you must pay for. Include only what you need and do not already own.
